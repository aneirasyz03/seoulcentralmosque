THE STORY

A Timeline of Seoul Central Mosque

1950s

Early Muslim community

Islam began developing again in Korea after the Korean War, with Turkish military personnel among the early influences. The Korean Muslim Society became a precursor to the Korean Muslim Federation (KMF).

1969

Land grant in Itaewon

A site of approximately 5,000 m² in Itaewon (Hannam-dong) was provided to support the growing Muslim community and international relations.

1974

Construction begins

Physical construction began in October 1974 with international Muslim support, notably from Saudi Arabia and other countries.

21 May 1976

Official opening

Seoul Central Mosque was officially opened and became the principal centre for Muslim life, education, and community activities in Seoul.

1990

Expansion

The complex was expanded with additional educational and community facilities.

Historical claims are based on publicly available sources. Verify with the Korean Muslim Federation or Seoul Central Mosque before formal publication.
